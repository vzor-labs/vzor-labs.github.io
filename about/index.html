About

Security work that ends in evidence.

Most security requests don't start with curiosity. They start with an insurer's questionnaire, an auditor's checklist or a customer's procurement form — and what those people need is proof, not a PDF of scanner output. Every VZOR engagement is built backwards from that moment: findings fixed, fixes retested, and an attestation letter and evidence pack written in the language underwriters, auditors and procurement teams actually read.

The same discipline runs on our own systems: our AI agents watch them every week, write down what they find, and leave the decision to a person. In every engagement the judgment, the production changes and the signature on the letter stay human — that's what makes the paperwork worth something. How each agent works
