About

A small practice, built to be met.

VZOR is a two-to-three-person application security practice based in Toronto. We keep the team small on purpose: the person who scopes your engagement is the person who tests, fixes alongside your developers, and signs the letter at the end.

What makes a small team enough is the tooling behind it. We built our own AI cyber security agents to do the reconnaissance, clustering, drafting and evidence mapping that used to need a bench of analysts. The judgment, the production changes and the signatures stay human.
