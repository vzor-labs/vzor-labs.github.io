How an engagement runs

  1. Discovery and conflict screen. Twenty minutes to confirm scope, authorization and that nothing puts us in conflict.
  2. Written authorization. Engagement letter and rules of engagement signed by both sides before any testing.
  3. Find, fix, retest. Named practitioner, agent-assisted, on a dated plan with a report due date and a retest date.
  4. Attestation. The letter is issued only after the retest record exists. Evidence hashes included.
